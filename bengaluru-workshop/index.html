Workshop in Bengaluru, March 2025

Open in its own page ↗

Curatorial note

Interdisciplinary academic workshop in Bengaluru in March 2025 that explored contemporary concerns in data labour from India. The workshop was organized by the University of Amsterdam’s Institute for Information Law, Tattle, and Aapti Institute.
