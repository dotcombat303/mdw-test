Debarun Sarkar, Cheshta Arora, Gabriele de Seta, and Luke Drozd

Artist

Debarun Sarkar is Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Digital Culture with the ALGOFOLK project at the Centre for Digital Narrative, Department of Literature, Linguistics and Aesthetic Studies at the University of Bergen.

Cheshta Arora is a Senior Researcher with the Technology & Society group at the Western Norway Research Institute, Sogndal.

Gabriele De Seta is a Researcher and the Project Leader of the ALGOFOLK project at the Centre for Digital Narrative, Department of Literature, Linguistics and Aesthetic Studies at the University of Bergen.

Luke Drozd is a UK born artist and maker currently based in Bergen, Norway.
