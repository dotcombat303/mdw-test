Yatharth

Artist

Yatharth is a researcher, designer and artist based in Bengaluru, India. His research practice explores caste on digital platforms and design injustice in digital technologies. Yatharth has worked with various nonprofits, research institutes, design schools and studios in the roles of media educator, qualitative researcher, curator, creative coder and designer.
